Supplementary Table 1. Summary of ChIP-seq sample metadata for CEBPA, IRF8, KLF4, and CIITA. This table summarizes the detailed metadata of all available ChIP-seq samples for the four transcription factors within the Transcriptional Regulator (TR) database, including sample ID, biological source (cell line or tissue name), source database (Cistrome DB, ChIP-Atlas, ENCODE, GTRD, ReMap), GEO/ENA accession number, peak count, and UDHS (Unified DNase I Hypersensitive Sites) coverage, comprising a total of 60 samples for CEBPA, 3 samples for IRF8, 21 samples for KLF4, and 6 samples for CIITA.

Supplementary Table 2. Metadata of PBMC ATAC-seq and H3K27ac super-enhancer samples. The table incorporates 20 PBMC-derived ATAC-seq pseudo-bulk samples retrieved from the ATACdb 2.0 database, spanning three GEO datasets: GSE101609 , GSE69749 (6 samples of normal peripheral blood mononuclear cells), and GSE114487 (2 samples of CRL7250 fibroblasts). A total of 1,461,241 consensus ATAC-seq peaks were obtained by merging all samples. Metadata of PBMC H3K27ac super-enhancer samples. The sample consists of primary adult naive CD4⁺CD25⁻CD45RA⁺CD27⁺ T cells, with H3K27ac ChIP-seq data sourced from NCBI GEO/SRA (accession: GSM2991633). A total of 585 super-enhancer regions were identified using the hg38 reference genome.

Supplementary Table 3. Metadata of ZNF146 ChIP-seq samples used in this study. The table incorporates five publicly available ZNF146 ChIP-seq peak sets aggregated via the TRAPT integrated transcription factor binding database (https://bioinfo.uth.edu/TRAPT/), spanning three independent sources: ENCODE (1 HEK293 sample, GSM2423862), Cistrome DB (3 HEK293 samples: GSM2026805, GSM2533946, GSM2421605), and GTRD (1 K562 sample, GSM3634337). A total of 147,567 consensus ChIP-seq peaks were obtained across all samples. UDHS coverage values (range 0.137–0.338) indicate all peak sets meet quality thresholds suitable for regulatory intersection analysis. BRCA-specific regulatory regions were defined by the union of MCF-7 ATAC-seq open chromatin peaks (GSM2740044, Sample_H_0541) and MCF-7 H3K27ac super-enhancer regions (ChIP: GSM4287400; input: GSM4287407, SE_02_2659), yielding 225 merged regulatory elements (hg38).

Supplementary Table 4. Metadata of transcription factor ChIP-seq samples and ESCC-specific regulatory regions used in this study. The table incorporates publicly available ChIP-seq peak sets for nine transcription factors (Group 1: MAFF, FOXO3, JUN, CDC5L, KLF5, FOSL2; Group 2: TP63, TFAP2C, TFAP2A). For each transcription factor, three independent ChIP-seq samples were selected to generate a merged consensus peak set (see Supplementary Table 8 for sample IDs). ESCC-specific regulatory regions were defined by ATAC-seq open chromatin peaks from sample H_2188 (1,229 peaks, hg38). Each transcription factor consensus peak set was intersected with these ESCC-specific ATAC-seq peaks to retain only cancer-cell-active binding sites, yielding between 11 (TP63) and 107 (FOXO3) ESCC-specific binding regions per transcription factor. All genomic coordinates are reported in hg38.
